01 — About

About TEDx
Infinity

TEDx Infinity is an independent TEDx platform dedicated to bringing together engineers, innovators, entrepreneurs, researchers, creators, and changemakers who are shaping the future through ideas worth spreading.

The name "Infinity" represents limitless curiosity, continuous learning, and the endless potential of human imagination. We believe that every breakthrough begins with a question, every innovation starts with an idea.

🎯
Mission
Create meaningful TEDx experiences that inspire people to think differently, act courageously, and build a better future.
🔭
Vision
Become one of the leading TEDx communities in the region, connecting future leaders, innovators, and dreamers.
∞
Why Infinity?
Endless possibilities, lifelong learning, continuous innovation, and human creativity without boundaries.
💡
Ideas Worth Spreading
Every transformation begins with a conversation. We create the stage for visionary thinkers to inspire.
